About

Bo Kaung

Builder / Breaker / Defender

I work at the intersection of AI agents and application security. The question I keep returning to is simple: what can this thing do to my machine? Most of my work is about answering it with evidence instead of vibes.

That has meant building SkillTriage, a static auditor for agent skills and Claude Code plugins, and mapping the OWASP lists into an offline atlas. It has also meant writing down the sessions that produced them — including the clean scan that scanned nothing, and the rule that failed before it worked.

What I write about

  • AI agent security: skills, plugins, MCP servers, hooks, and supply chain.
  • Static analysis: Semgrep, SARIF, policy gates, and the limits of a clean result.
  • Evidence: reproducible runs, raw output, and what a tool does not prove.
